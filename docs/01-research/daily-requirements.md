# Daily Nutritional Requirements and Recommended Dose

**Phase:** 01 Research
**Status:** Complete
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-17

---

## Summary

The target daily intake of broccoli microgreens is **30–60 g fresh weight per day** for general health and preventive benefits, with a therapeutic range of **113–142 g/day** for specific disease management. The primary dosing metric is sulforaphane (SFN): clinical research supports 50–150 µmol/day as a safe, effective range for healthy adults. At ~6.4–7.2 µmol SFN potential per gram of fresh microgreens, this corresponds to roughly 7–23 g of fresh microgreens per day for the low end, and 64–100 g for the therapeutic end.

**Device design target: 30 g/day** — sufficient for ~200 µmol sulforaphane daily, within the well-studied beneficial range, and achievable with a compact rotating tray system.

---

## Sulforaphane as the Primary Dose Metric

Broccoli microgreens are grown for sulforaphane above all other nutrients. Vitamins C, E, K, and folate are also well-supplied, but sulforaphane is the compound with the strongest and most studied health evidence, and it is essentially unique to the Brassica family.

### What Sulforaphane Does

- **Nrf2 pathway activation:** Triggers the body's own antioxidant and detoxification enzymes (phase 2 enzymes). This is dose-dependent but active at low doses.
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Suppresses NF-κB signaling pathway.
- **Detoxification:** Enhances elimination of environmental carcinogens and pollutants (e.g., air pollution compounds). A study of Qidong, China residents showed measurable increases in benzene/acrolein excretion at 40 µmol SFN + 600 µmol GRN daily.
- **Chemopreventive:** Inhibits initiation and progression of certain cancers in animal models and some human trials.
- **Neuroprotective:** Studied in autism spectrum disorder (ASD) trials at 50–150 µmol/day.
- **Metabolic:** Reduced fasting blood glucose in type 2 diabetics at ~153 µmol/day over 12 weeks.

## Clinical Dose Ranges

| Context | SFN Dose (µmol/day) | Approximate Fresh Microgreens |
|---------|---------------------|-------------------------------|
| Validated bioavailability (clinical study) | 100 µmol | **~16 g** |
| General health / Nrf2 activation | 50–100 µmol | **8–16 g** |
| Detoxification (pollutant elimination) | 40–150 µmol | **6–23 g** |
| ASD symptom management | 50–150 µmol | **8–23 g** |
| Type 2 diabetes blood glucose | ~153 µmol | **~24 g** |
| Therapeutic / cancer prevention range | 150–400 µmol | **23–64 g** |
| Therapeutic (aggressive / practitioner-supervised) | 400+ µmol | **64+ g (4–5 oz)** |

**Conversion used:** 6.4 µmol SFN potential per gram fresh broccoli microgreens (conservative, from clinical study data). Actual yield depends on chewing efficiency, myrosinase activity, and bioavailability (~50% urinary recovery documented).

## Vitamin Coverage from a 30g Daily Serving

| Nutrient | Amount in 30g | % Adult RDA | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------------|-------|
| Sulforaphane potential | ~192–216 µmol | — | Well within beneficial clinical range |
| Vitamin C | ~35 mg | ~39% | Supplements the diet meaningfully |
| Vitamin E | ~7.2 mg | ~48% | Significant contribution |
| Vitamin K | ~39 µg | ~33–43% | Meaningful; high-dose warfarin users should note |
| Folate | ~32 µg | ~8% | Modest; not a primary source |
| Potassium | ~127 mg | ~3% | Minor contribution |

**Conclusion:** A 30g daily serving covers approximately 40–50% of vitamin E and C needs, and delivers a clinically meaningful dose of sulforaphane. Broccoli microgreens should be viewed as a **health-optimizing superfood supplement**, not a complete nutritional replacement.

## Recommended Daily Target for This Device

**Primary target: 30 g/day fresh broccoli microgreens**

Rationale:
- Delivers ~192–216 µmol sulforaphane potential — in the upper portion of the well-studied clinical range
- Clinically validated dose is 16g (100 µmol); 30g provides meaningful safety margin
- Practically achievable: a 10×20 tray yields ~200–325g in 7–10 days, so 30g/day requires ~one tray per 7–10 days
- Palatable volume: 30g raw greens ≈ a large handful, easy to incorporate into a smoothie
- Conservative relative to the 113–142g/day "therapeutic" ceiling; allows user to scale up

**Secondary target (therapeutic mode): 60–100 g/day**

This requires approximately doubling tray capacity and is appropriate if the device is used with a specific health goal (e.g., cancer prevention, metabolic support). See [production-targets.md](production-targets.md) for tray math at both scales.

| Nutrient | Gap | Recommended Source |
|----------|-----|-------------------|

| Iron | Partial | Red meat, legumes |
| Vitamin B12 | None | Animal products or supplements |
| Omega-3 fatty acids | None | Fish, flaxseed, walnuts |
| Vitamin D | None | Sun exposure, fortified foods, supplements |

---

## Frequency

Daily consumption is recommended. Sulforaphane has a half-life of hours and does not accumulate in the body; its benefits derive from consistent, regular exposure rather than periodic large doses. Steady daily intake at moderate dose outperforms occasional high-dose consumption.
