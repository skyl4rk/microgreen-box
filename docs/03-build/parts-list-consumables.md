## Sourcing Notes

### Seeds
- **True Leaf Market** (trueLeafMarket.com): Best quality US source; sells by weight; broccoli microgreen seed $7–15/lb
- **Johnny's Selected Seeds**: High-quality commercial seed; broccoli sprouting/microgreen varieties
- **AliExpress** (bulk): Very low cost ($5–10/kg) but variable quality and longer shipping; useful for prototype
- **Mountain Rose Herbs**: Organic options

### Coir
- Any garden centre carries compressed coir pucks (marketed as "seed starter pellets" or "coir pots")
- Jiffy-7 peat pellets are NOT coir — check label; look for 100% coconut coir
- Hydroponic supply stores carry coir bricks at the best price per gram

### CalMag
- Available at any hydroponics shop
- General Hydroponics CALiMAGic: widely available; proven formulation
- Can omit entirely — device produces nutritious microgreens on coir alone; sulforaphane is unaffected by mineral supplement
